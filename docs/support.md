# Support

Support is provided on a best-effort basis through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ApexAspire/ApexGauge/issues). There is no response-time or resolution SLA.

For private enquiries, email [admin@apexaspire.co.uk](mailto:admin@apexaspire.co.uk).

## Reporting a bug

Before opening an issue, check the [service status](https://apexaspire.github.io/ApexGauge/status.json). Include:

- the iPhone and Apple Watch models involved
- the iOS and watchOS versions
- the affected provider — Claude, Codex or Kimi
- what you expected and what happened
- steps that reproduce the problem
- a screenshot of **Settings → Watch**, including **Last push**

Remove credentials, QR codes and other private information from screenshots and logs before posting them.

## Provider endpoint changes

Apex Gauge is unofficial and depends on provider endpoints that may change without notice. If an endpoint breaks, the app will show an error instead of current quota data. We will flag the affected provider in `status.json` and ship a fix when possible.
